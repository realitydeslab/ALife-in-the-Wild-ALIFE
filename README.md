# Artificial Life in the Wild

Invited full paper for the **Artificial Life Journal** (MIT Press).

Expanding the published ALIFE 2025 conference paper: ["Spore in the Wild"](https://github.com/realitydeslab/Spore-in-the-Wild-ALIFE-2025).

## Authors
- Botao Amber Hu (University of Oxford / Reality Design Lab)
- Helena Rong (NYU Shanghai)

## Abstract
ALife has pursued Open-Ended Evolution in laboratories for 35 years. Infrastructural sovereignty — blockchain, TEEs, DePIN — now enables artificial life in the wild. This paper presents the first longitudinal field study of wild autonomous agents (Spore.fun), combining digital ethnography with quantitative on-chain aliveness analysis. We observe cultural speciation, co-evolutionary arms races, and a 61-day Cambrian explosion with 6.7% survival rate. We call for Agent Ethology as the science of studying artificial life in the wild.
